# Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI and Excel** to analyze sales performance, revenue streams, profitability, product categories, regional performance, and sales channels.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional sales data into meaningful **business insights and actionable recommendations** for improving sales, profitability, inventory allocation, and digital-channel performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project provides an end-to-end analysis of Adidas retail sales performance across different:

- Regions and states
- Product categories
- Sales channels
- Sales methods
- Pricing ranges
- Time periods

The dashboard tracks important business KPIs such as **Total Sales, Total Units Sold, Total Profit, Profit Margin, and Average Price per Unit**.

## 📊 Data Source

- **Source:** Kaggle – Adidas US Sales Dataset
- **Dataset Size:** 9,600+ transaction records
- **Time Period:** 2020–2021
- **Geographical Coverage:** Multiple US regions and states
- **Data Includes:**
  - Retailer information
  - Product categories
  - Product pricing
  - Units sold
  - Sales revenue
  - Operating profit
  - Operating margin
  - Sales method
  - Sales channel
  - Regional information

## 🚀 Features & Highlights

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides an overview of the overall business performance through:

- **Total Sales** – Total revenue generated from all transactions.
- **Total Units Sold** – Total quantity of products sold.
- **Total Profit** – Total operating profit generated.
- **Profit Margin (%)** – Measures profitability relative to sales.
- **Average Price per Unit** – Shows the average selling price of products.

---

### 2. Regional Sales Analysis

The dashboard provides a detailed comparison of sales performance across different regions and states.

This helps identify:

- Top-performing regions
- Low-performing markets
- Regional revenue differences
- Opportunities for inventory optimization
- Areas requiring promotional strategies

---

### 3. Product Category Analysis

Product performance is analyzed across different Adidas product categories.

The analysis helps determine:

- Best-selling product categories
- Revenue contribution by product
- Unit sales by category
- High-performing footwear and apparel segments
- Potential growth opportunities

---

### 4. Sales Channel & Method Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales performance across different channels and methods, including:

- **In-store**
- **Online**
- **Outlet**

This provides insights into:

- Revenue contribution by channel
- Profitability of different sales methods
- Customer purchasing patterns
- Opportunities for digital-channel growth

---

### 5. Time-Series & Pricing Analysis

The dashboard provides dynamic analysis of sales trends over time and across different price ranges.

It helps identify:

- Monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Seasonal demand patterns
- Peak sales periods
- Pricing ranges with higher transaction volumes
- Changes in revenue and profitability over time
